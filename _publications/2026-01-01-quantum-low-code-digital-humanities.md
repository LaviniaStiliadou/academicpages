abstract: |
  This work, presented at DH2026, examines how quantum computing can be made accessible to the digital humanities. Quantum applications are typically implemented using provider-specific software development kits, such as Qiskit, which can create a significant programming barrier for researchers without a technical background. To address this challenge, we developed Qonstruct, a low-code platform that enables digital humanities researchers to perform quantum computing tasks such as clustering on quantum computers without requiring extensive programming knowledge.
