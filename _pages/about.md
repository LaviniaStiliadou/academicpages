I am a researcher in **quantum software engineering** at the University of Stuttgart. My work focuses on one question: *how can people without a background in physics or quantum programming still build and use quantum applications?*

What drives me
======
I have a real passion for what I do. To me, quantum computing is about **breaking walls**: the walls between experts and beginners, between disciplines, and between complex theory and practical use. I am **goal-oriented** and like to see ideas through from the first concept to a working result. I am also convinced that **a strong team always leads to success**. Most of my work grew out of close collaboration, and I enjoy bringing people with different backgrounds together.

Research interests
======
- **Low-code quantum computing.** Together with colleagues, I develop *Qonstruct*, an open-source, provider-agnostic low-code platform. With it, users model quantum algorithms visually and run them as workflows in hybrid cloud environments, without writing SDK code. Recent work extends it with real-time collaboration, experience-aware interfaces, and domain-specific elements for fields such as finance, logistics, and the digital humanities.
- **Patterns for quantum computing.** I capture proven solutions as patterns, for example for composing quantum algorithms and for quantum machine learning. Two of these papers received Best Paper Awards (IQSOFT 2025, PATTERNS 2025).
- **Variational quantum algorithms.** I study the cost landscapes of hybrid quantum-classical algorithms and what they mean for optimization in practice.
